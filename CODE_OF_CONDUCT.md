# Code of conduct

## The short version

People arrive here mid-incident, stressed, often having just discovered that
their credentials are gone. Answer the question they asked. Do not lecture them
about how they got infected.

## Expected

- Be direct and specific. "Run `./ci/selftest.sh` and paste the output" beats
  "have you tried debugging it".
- Assume the person on the other end is competent and short of time.
- Keep incident details out of public issues. Redact organization names,
  repository names, usernames and internal URLs before you paste anything.
- Disagree about the technical substance, not about the person.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Blaming or shaming someone for being compromised. This campaign has hit over a
  thousand repository owners.
- Posting anyone's private information, or another organization's incident
  details, without their consent.
- Using an issue thread to advertise a product.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock or delete any contribution that breaks these rules,
and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem privately through the
repository's **Report content** control, or through the Security tab if it
involves a vulnerability.
